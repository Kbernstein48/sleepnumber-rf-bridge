Use the position control to raise the head, then adjust this angle to match the bed. The calibration changes the drawing.

Settings and saved positions persist after restart. The bridge reads the bed's actual position on startup.

Local API:

POST /api/v1/bed/position {"value":40}
POST /api/v1/bed/stop {}
POST /api/v1/bed/light {"enabled":true}

Stop withdraws motion keepalive and confirms that the motors have stopped.
